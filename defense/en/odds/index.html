Recruit Odds — Chui Defense

One recruit ticket gives one random crew shard (10 tickets = 10 shards). You get recruit tickets by clearing a stage for the first time, and you can also buy them with gold or in the shop.

  • The grade is decided first (Common 50% · Rare 30% · Epic 15% · Legendary 5%), and every crew within a grade has the same chance.
  • Mythic crews are not in the recruit pool (combine them during a run with their recipe).
  • These are the same values as Recruit → Odds per Crew in the game. This page is updated when crews are added.
  • Korean: https://chuifather.github.io/defense/odds/
