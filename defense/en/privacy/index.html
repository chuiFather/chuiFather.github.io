Privacy Policy — Chui Defense

Effective date: 💡 launch date · Last updated: October 17, 2026 · URL: https://chuifather.github.io/defense/en/privacy/ (Korean original: https://chuifather.github.io/defense/privacy/ — docs/privacy-policy.md. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.)

The developer of "Chui Defense" (the "Game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how.

1. Personal information the developer collects

None. The developer does not collect or store on any server information that identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. Game progress (gold, recruit tickets, crew stars and levels, stage records, settings, etc.) is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer. Once you tap Play Games cloud save or load from the cloud, the same data is also saved automatically to your Google account storage (Google Play Games saved games) when you leave the Game (at most once every 10 minutes). If Android device backup is on, Android also backs up the game data to your Google account backup (Google Drive) and can restore it when you reinstall the app.

3. Retention and deletion (how to delete your data)

The developer keeps no user data on servers, so you can delete it yourself, immediately, as follows. If you need help, email us (section 6).

  • Game data on your device: uninstall the app, or go to device Settings → Apps → Chui Defense → Storage → Clear data.
  • Play Games cloud save: delete this game's data in the Play Games app (or Google Account → Data & privacy → Play Games).
  • Google account backup: manage or delete it in device Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Advertising information: reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Information kept by third-party services follows each service's own policy.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

5. Your rights

You can stop all processing at any time by uninstalling the app, resetting your advertising ID or turning off personalized ads, or disconnecting Play Games.
