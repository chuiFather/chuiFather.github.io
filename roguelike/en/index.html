Chui Expedition

A turn-based card adventure with a party of pups · Developer Black and White Raccoon (chuiFather)

Chui and three friends line up in a row and pick their way down a branching map through 3 acts in this turn-based card roguelike. Every friend can only play cards from certain spots in the line, so the order matters. Collect cards and snack-and-toy relics through battles, events, shops and rest stops. Three worlds — fantasy dungeon, martial arts and space — 29 friends, and battles that change with your party's mood. After each run, spend bones in the village doghouse to hire and train friends.
