Privacy Policy — Chui Expedition

Effective: October 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026 (push notifications added) · Posted at: https://chuifather.github.io/roguelike/en/privacy/

The developer (the "Developer") of "Chui Expedition" (the "Game") values your privacy and follows applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how. (The Korean version is the original; if the two differ, the Korean version applies.)

1. Personal information the Developer collects directly

None. The Developer does not directly collect or store on any server information that identifies you, such as your name, email or phone number.
Game progress (bones, hired and trained friends, achievements, lifetime records, the run in progress, settings, etc.) is stored on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers. If Android backup (Google account backup) is turned on for your device, this data is also kept in your own Google backup and may be restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new device. Google keeps this backup in your account; the Developer cannot see it.
If you sign in to Play Games, a copy of your game progress may be kept in Play Games cloud save (Saved Games) under your own Google account. Google keeps this copy as well; the Developer cannot see it.

2-1. Push notifications

  • Game reminders (daily gift, continue your saved run): scheduled and shown on the device only; nothing is sent out.
  • Update and notice notifications: received through Firebase Cloud Messaging (see the table above).
  • Event and offer notifications (promotional): sent only if you opt in. They are off by default; the game shows you the result of opting in or out (date and sender) and asks again every two years. They are not sent between 9 PM and 8 AM, their title starts with "(Ad)", and you can opt out anytime in Settings → "Event & offer alerts".
  • All notifications can be turned off in Settings → "Push notifications" or in your device settings.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Game data stored on your device: deleted from the device when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Any copy in your Google backup can be removed in device settings (Google → Backup).
  • Play Games achievements, leaderboards and cloud save: can be removed from your Google account (see "How to delete your data").
  • Information kept by third-party services follows each service's policy.

How to delete your data

The Developer's servers hold no user data, so you can delete it yourself as below. If you need help, email us (section 6).

  • Game progress: device Settings → Apps → Chui Expedition → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app)
  • Google backup copy: device Settings → Google → Backup
  • Play Games records (achievements, leaderboards, cloud save): Play Games app → Settings → delete game data or disconnect, or your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com) → Data & privacy → delete Play Games data
  • Advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads → reset or delete

4. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

5. Your rights

You can stop any processing at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data, resetting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization, changing your ad consent, or disconnecting Play Games.
