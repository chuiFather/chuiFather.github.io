Privacy Policy — Chui's Space Station

Effective: November 6, 2026 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

The developer ("Developer") of "Chui's Space Station" (the "Game") values your privacy and follows the applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how. (Korean version: https://chuifather.github.io/privacy/)

1. Personal information the Developer collects directly

None. The Developer does not directly collect or store on a server any information that identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number.

Game progress (resources, crew, settings, etc.) is stored on your device and is not sent to the Developer. - If Android backup is on, game data may be included in your own Google account backup (handled by Google; the Developer cannot see it). - If you sign in to Play Games, your cloud save is stored in your Play Games account (see section 2). - A code made with "Copy backup code" in Settings is kept by you.

3. Retention and deletion (how to delete your data)

The Developer keeps no user data on a server, so you can delete it yourself, right away, as follows. If you need help, email us (section 6).

  • Game data on your device: deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or tap "Start a new game" in Settings.
  • Play Games cloud save: delete this game's data in the Play Games app (or Google Account → Data & privacy → Play Games).
  • Google account backup: manage or delete it in your device settings → Google → Backup.
  • Ad-related information: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Information kept by third-party services follows each service's policy.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

5. Your rights

You can stop any processing at any time by uninstalling the app, resetting your advertising ID or turning off personalized ads, or disconnecting Play Games. For questions, contact us below.
