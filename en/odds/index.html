Recruit Odds — Chui's Space Station

One Recruit Ticket recruits one crew (10 tickets = 11 crew). Tickets refill over time in the game and can also be bought in the shop.

  • A rarity is drawn first (Common 52% · Rare 30% · Epic 13% · Legendary 5%), and every crew within that rarity has the same chance.
  • Crew already at 5 stars (★5) leave the pool, which raises the odds of the other crew of the same rarity. In the game, Recruit → "See odds per crew" shows the odds for your current progress.
  • Event and collab limited crew never appear in recruits.
  • The tables below are for a fresh start with no ★5 crew and the 117 crew released so far. This page is updated when crew are added.
