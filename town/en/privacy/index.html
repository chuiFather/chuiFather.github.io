Privacy Policy — Toasty Chui Cafe

Effective: release date · Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Posted at: https://chuifather.github.io/town/en/privacy/

The developer ("we") of "Toasty Chui Cafe" (the "game") respects your privacy and follows applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the game handles and how.

1. Information we collect directly

None. We do not collect or store names, emails, phone numbers or any other information that identifies you. There are no accounts.

Game progress (coins, cafe level and reputation, known menus and recipe hints, ingredient stock, stations, staff, furniture and decor, farm plots, check-ins, rewarded-ad counts, settings) is stored on your device and is not sent to our servers.

  • If Android backup (Google account backup) is on, game data may be included in your own Google backup and restored when you reinstall or move to a new device. Google keeps this backup in your account; we cannot see it.
  • If you sign in to Play Games and use cloud save, a copy of your progress is stored in your Play Games account (saved games, see section 2). Google keeps this copy; we cannot see it.
  • "Backup code" → Copy only turns your progress into text on your device clipboard; it is never sent to us.

2-1. Push notifications

  • Game reminders (crops ready, "time to open the cafe" in the evening on days you haven't picked up your check-in gift) are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Update and notice notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (table above).
  • Event & offer notifications (promotional) are sent only if you agree. They are off by default; we show you the result (date and sender) whenever you agree or refuse, and ask again every two years. They are never sent between 9 PM and 8 AM, their titles start with "(Ad)", and you can refuse anytime in Settings → "Event & offer alerts".
  • Turn off all notifications in Settings → "Push notifications" or in your device settings; this also unsubscribes the notification topics. On Android 13 and later the game asks for notification permission once, after your first day of business.

3. Retention and deletion (how to delete your data)

We keep no user data on servers, so you can delete everything yourself, immediately. Email us (section 6) if you need help.

  • Game data on your device: uninstall the app, use Settings → "Reset data" in the game, or device Settings → Apps → Toasty Chui Cafe → Storage → Clear data.
  • Google account backup: device Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Play Games records (achievements, leaderboards, cloud save): delete this game's data or disconnect it in the Play Games app settings, or in your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com) → Data & privacy.
  • A backup code you copied: delete it wherever you pasted it.
  • Ad information: reset or delete your advertising ID in device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Data kept by third-party services follows their own policies.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

5. Your rights

You can stop all processing at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data, resetting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization, changing ad consent, turning off push notifications, or disconnecting Play Games.
