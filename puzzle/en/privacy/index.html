Privacy Policy — Chui's Puzzle Play

Effective: release date · Last updated: October 28, 2026 (push notifications, coupons)

The developer ("we") of "Chui's Puzzle Play" (the "game") respects your privacy and follows applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Information we collect directly

None. We do not collect or store names, emails, phone numbers or any other information that identifies you.

Game progress (stages, friend album, coins, items, settings) is stored on your device and is not sent to us. - If Android backup is on, game data may be included in your own Google account backup (handled by Google; we cannot see it). - If you sign in to Play Games, your cloud save is stored in your Play Games account (see section 2). - A "backup code" is text you copy yourself; it is never sent to us. - Coupon and collab codes are checked on your device; the list of codes you used is stored only with your game progress (device, backups, cloud save).

2-1. Push notifications

  • Game reminders (hearts full, Daily Challenge) are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Update and notice notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (table above).
  • Event & offer notifications (promotional) are sent only if you agree. They are off by default; we show you the result (date and sender) whenever you agree or refuse, and ask again every two years. They are never sent between 9 PM and 8 AM, their titles start with "(Ad)", and you can refuse anytime in Settings → General → "Event & offer alerts".
  • Turn off all notifications in Settings → General → "Push notifications" or in your device settings; this also unsubscribes the notification topics.

3. Retention and deletion

We keep no user data on servers, so you can delete everything yourself, immediately: - Game data on your device: uninstall the app, or Settings → "Reset data". - Google account backup: device Settings → Google → Backup. - Play Games cloud save: delete this game's data in the Play Games app (or Google Account → Data & privacy → Play Games). - Ad information: reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. - Data kept by third-party services follows their own policies.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
