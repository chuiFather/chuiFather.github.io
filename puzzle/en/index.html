Chui's Puzzle Play

Slide, match, sort, link and paint! Eight cozy puzzles with cute pet friends.

  • Friend Merge: slide and merge friends all the way up to Chui
  • Puzzle Park: match 3 toys to beat the boss (40 stages, then endless)
  • Puppy Houses: sort friends so each house holds 4 of a kind (40 stages, then endless)
  • Friend Lines: connect matching friends and fill the board (40 stages, then endless)
  • Chui's Walk Escape: slide the boxes to get Chui out the door (40 stages, then endless)
  • Friend Drop: drop friends into a jar and merge matching ones
  • Friend Blocks: place blocks, clear lines, chain combos
  • Friend Picture Belt: send paint pals around the belt to clear the pixels and reveal a friend (40 stages, then endless)
  • Daily Challenge: the same board for everyone, a new mode each day
