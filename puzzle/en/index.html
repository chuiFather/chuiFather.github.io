Chui's Puzzle Play

Slide, match, link, merge and place! Five cozy puzzles with cute pet friends.

  • Friend Merge: slide and merge friends all the way up to Chui
  • Puzzle Park: match 3 toys to beat the boss (40 stages)
  • Link Merge: draw a line through friends to merge them at once (40 stages)
  • Snack Shop: merge snacks and fill customer orders
  • Friend Blocks: place blocks, clear lines, chain combos
  • Daily Challenge: the same board for everyone, a new mode each day
