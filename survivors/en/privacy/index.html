Privacy Policy — Chui Survivors

Effective: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Posted at: https://chuifather.github.io/survivors/en/privacy/

The developer (the "Developer") of "Chui Survivors" (the "Game") values your privacy and follows applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how. (The Korean version is the original; if the two differ, the Korean version applies.)

1. Personal information the Developer collects directly

None. The Developer does not directly collect or store on any server information that identifies you, such as your name, email or phone number.
Game progress (allowance, permanent upgrades, achievements, settings, etc.) is stored on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers. If Android backup (Google account backup) is turned on for your device, this data is also kept in your own Google backup and may be restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new device. Google keeps this backup in your account; the Developer cannot see it.
Coupon and Chui Series collab codes are strings you type in yourself and are not sent to the Developer (the record of redeemed codes stays in the game data on your device).
Weekly ranked walk alerts (new week, 4 hours before closing) are scheduled on your device only; no information is sent or collected for them. You can turn them off in Settings → Game → "Ranked walk alerts" or in your device's app notification settings.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Game data stored on your device: deleted from the device when you uninstall the app. Any copy in your Google backup can be removed in device settings (Google → Backup).
  • Information kept by third-party services follows each service's policy.

How to delete your data

The Developer's servers hold no user data, so you can delete it yourself as below. If you need help, email us (section 6).

  • Game progress: device Settings → Apps → Chui Survivors → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app)
  • Google backup copy: device Settings → Google → Backup
  • Play Games records (achievements, leaderboards): Play Games app → Settings → delete game data or disconnect
  • Advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads → reset or delete

4. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

5. Your rights

You can stop any processing at any time by uninstalling the app, resetting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization, or disconnecting Play Games.
